Sample Feature

This page demonstrates how Announcements can be customized for your organization. Share organization-wide updates, event reminders, policy changes, emergency notifications, and important news with staff, volunteers, and participants.

📢 Organization News

Share important updates about programs, services, partnerships, and organizational achievements.

📅 Upcoming Events

Notify users about workshops, orientations, community events, trainings, and special activities.

🚨 Emergency Alerts

Quickly communicate urgent information such as office closures, weather updates, or emergency procedures.

📜 Policy Updates

Inform staff and participants about changes to organizational policies, procedures, or requirements.

🎉 Success Stories

Celebrate participant accomplishments, staff recognition, volunteer milestones, and community successes.

📨 Scheduled Announcements

Create announcements in advance and schedule them to be published automatically on selected dates.

👥 Audience Targeting

Choose whether announcements are visible to staff, volunteers, participants, or specific departments.

📎 Attachments

Include documents, flyers, forms, images, or links with announcements to provide additional information.

🔔 Notifications

Notify users when new announcements are posted so important information is not missed.

📊 Announcement History

Maintain an archive of previous announcements for future reference and organizational record keeping.


Why Announcements Matter

Keeping everyone informed strengthens communication, improves engagement, and ensures that staff, volunteers, and participants stay updated on important information. A New Me™ provides a centralized announcement system that can be customized for organizations of every size.
Every organization is unique. A New Me™ can be customized to meet your specific operational needs.
