Sample Feature

This page demonstrates how Organization Settings can be customized for your organization. Administrators can configure users, permissions, branding, notifications, security settings, and other options to create a portal that supports their specific operations.

🏢 Organization Profile

Manage your organization's name, logo, contact information, mission statement, and branding.

👥 User Management

Add, remove, or manage staff, volunteers, supervisors, and administrator accounts.

🔐 Roles & Permissions

Control what each user can view, edit, create, or manage throughout the portal.

🎨 Portal Branding

Customize logos, colors, banners, welcome messages, and other branding elements to match your organization.

🔔 Notification Preferences

Configure email alerts, in-app notifications, reminders, and communication settings for staff and participants.

📂 Program Configuration

Enable or disable modules, create custom programs, and tailor portal features to your organization's services.

🛡️ Security Settings

Manage passwords, account security, login preferences, and access controls to help protect organizational data.

💾 Data & Backups

Configure data retention preferences, exports, backups, and recovery options for organizational records.

📊 Dashboard Preferences

Customize dashboard widgets, reports, shortcuts, and home screen layouts for administrators and staff.

⚙️ System Integrations

Connect third-party services, calendars, communication tools, reporting platforms, and other supported integrations.


Why Organization Settings Matter

Every organization operates differently. A New Me™ is designed to adapt to those differences by allowing administrators to configure the platform around their programs, staff, workflows, and organizational goals. This flexibility helps create a portal that truly supports each organization's unique mission.
Every organization is unique. A New Me™ can be customized to meet your specific operational needs.
