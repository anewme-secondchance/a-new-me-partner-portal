Sample Feature

This page demonstrates how Participant Management can be customized for your organization. Every organization has different workflows, reporting requirements, and participant needs. The features below are examples and can be tailored to fit your programs and services.

🔍 Search Participants

Quickly locate participants by name, ID number, phone number, email, or assigned case manager.

👤 View Participant Profiles

Access participant information including contact details, goals, services received, and important milestones.

📈 Track Progress

Monitor participant progress toward employment, housing, education, financial stability, and personal goals.

👥 Assign Case Managers

Assign participants to staff members, mentors, volunteers, or case managers for ongoing support.

📝 Notes & Case History

Document meetings, referrals, phone calls, follow-ups, and important updates for each participant.

📄 Upload Documents

Store resumes, identification documents, certificates, housing paperwork, employment forms, and other important files.

✅ Goals Completed

View completed goals, active goals, and upcoming milestones to celebrate participant success.

💼 Employment Status

Track job readiness, applications, interviews, employment placement, and employment retention.

🏠 Housing Status

Monitor emergency housing, transitional housing, permanent housing, and housing assistance progress.

🎓 Education Progress

Track GED completion, college enrollment, workforce training, certifications, and educational achievements.

📅 Upcoming Appointments

View scheduled meetings, classes, counseling sessions, probation appointments, interviews, and important events.


Why This Matters

Participant Management provides organizations with a centralized location to monitor participant progress, coordinate services, improve communication, measure program outcomes, and better support individuals throughout their reentry journey.
Every organization is unique. A New Me™ can be customized to meet your specific operational needs.
