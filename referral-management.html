Sample Feature

This page demonstrates how Referral Management can be customized for your organization. Referral workflows, community partners, approval processes, and tracking tools can all be tailored to support your programs and services.

🤝 Create Referrals

Refer participants to housing providers, employers, healthcare providers, educational programs, food assistance, transportation services, legal aid, and other community resources.

🏢 Community Partners

Maintain a directory of trusted organizations, agencies, nonprofits, businesses, and government programs that provide services to participants.

📋 Track Referral Status

Monitor referrals from creation through completion, including pending, accepted, scheduled, completed, or declined referrals.

📅 Appointment Scheduling

Record appointment dates, locations, contact information, and follow-up reminders related to each referral.

📞 Follow-Up Tracking

Document participant progress after referrals and ensure services were received successfully.

📝 Referral Notes

Allow staff to record conversations, updates, barriers, and additional information related to participant referrals.

📊 Referral History

View a complete history of referrals made for each participant to better coordinate services and avoid duplicate referrals.

🔔 Referral Notifications

Notify staff when referrals are created, updated, completed, or require follow-up actions.

📈 Referral Reports

Generate reports showing referral activity, service utilization, community partnerships, and participant outcomes.

🌎 Resource Directory

Maintain an up-to-date directory of local and regional resources available to participants, making it easier for staff to connect individuals with support.


Why Referral Management Matters

Organizations often work with many community partners to help participants access housing, employment, education, healthcare, transportation, legal services, and other essential resources. Referral Management helps staff coordinate services, monitor participant progress, strengthen community partnerships, and improve overall program outcomes through one centralized platform.
Every organization is unique. A New Me™ can be customized to meet your specific operational needs.
