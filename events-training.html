Create and manage workshops, orientations, classes, community events, support groups, and special programs.

Track attendance to measure participation, engagement, and program completion.

Coordinate volunteer orientations, required training, and ongoing educational opportunities.

📢 Event Announcements

Share upcoming events, reminders, schedule changes, and important updates with participants and staff.

Track completed trainings and issue certificates for workshops, classes, or organizational requirements.

Why Events & Training Matter

Events and training opportunities help organizations educate participants, strengthen staff, engage volunteers, and build stronger communities. A New Me™ provides one place to organize, promote, manage, and evaluate these activities while supporting your organization's mission.
Every organization is unique. A New Me™ can be customized to meet your specific operational needs.
