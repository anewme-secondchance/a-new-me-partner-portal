📊 Participant Limits

A New Me™ automatically tracks active participants within your organization account.

  • Small Plan: Up to 25 Active Participants
  • Medium Plan: Up to 100 Active Participants
  • Large Plan: Up to 250 Active Participants

When an organization reaches its participant limit, the system will notify staff and provide an option to upgrade.


Example:

A Small Organization has 25 active participants. When staff attempts to add Participant #26, the system displays:

Participant Limit Reached

Your organization has reached the maximum number of active participants allowed under your current subscription plan. Please upgrade your plan to continue adding participants.
